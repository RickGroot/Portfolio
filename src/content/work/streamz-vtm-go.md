From 2022 to 2024 I worked on the Smart TV apps of Streamz and VTM GO, two Belgian streaming
services, for DPG Media. VTM GO is free to watch; Streamz is a subscription. Both ran from one
white-label TV app in a monorepo. Both live on the TV in the living room, so the work was
the app on the TV itself, from browsing to playback, kept quick on the TVs people actually own.
