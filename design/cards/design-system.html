Rules

  • CornersRadius 0. Only records, reels and dots are round.
  • LineOne 2px ink rule for every border and divider.
  • ShadowHard 6px offset, no blur. A card lifts by moving, not by glowing.
  • FieldOne flat pigment per card. Never a gradient.
  • PhotosPrinted in ink on the field, or shown plain inside a window.
  • GrainOne paper-grain layer over the whole page.
  • NumberEvery card has a catalogue number (RG 001…).

Three directions pick one

Same cards, three ways to lay them out. All three fit the whole portfolio in about one screen plus the card backs.

A · The table

Cards are dealt loose onto a table, a little crooked. You can drag them around; picking one up flips it. Most playful and most “alive”. Needs a tidy fallback grid on phones.

B · The crate

One row you flick through like records in a crate. The card in front is large, the rest show their spines. Smallest, and the best on a phone. You see one card at a time.

C · The wall

A tight grid that fills the screen, every card a different size, all moving a little. Everything visible at once, easiest to build well. Least surprising of the three.
